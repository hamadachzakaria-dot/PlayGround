# ADDENDUM TO THE MASTER DIRECTIVE — FINAL GOAL, REVENUE LOGIC & PROMPT LIBRARY
Append this after the existing MASTER DIRECTIVE. It adds a concrete end goal, translates it into catalog math, layers a feedback loop onto the existing 2-day cadence, and supplies ready-to-use art-generation prompts.
==================================================
ADDENDUM 1 — FINAL GOAL (NORTH STAR)
FINAL GOAL:
Build this coloring-book catalog into a source of RECURRING net royalty income of approximately $1,000 USD/month, sustained for at least 2 consecutive months — not a single lucky spike from one launch.
Every other section of the Master Directive is a tool serving this target, not an end in itself. Page count, cadence, and catalog size are only "correct" if they are actually moving the catalog toward this number.
This is a target that shapes priorities, not a guaranteed outcome. No specific book, niche, price, or keyword set can promise sales volume — track real data and adjust rather than assuming any plan will work as designed.
==================================================
ADDENDUM 2 — TRANSLATING $1,000/MONTH INTO CATALOG MATH
Baseline: current (Sept 2026) KDP paperback royalty mechanics, US marketplace, black & white interior. Re-verify exact numbers with KDP's own Printing Cost & Royalty Calculator before every publish (Section 19 still applies).
CURRENT KDP PAPERBACK ROYALTY RULES:
Royalty rate: 60% of list price if list price ≥ $9.99; 50% if below $9.99 (threshold effective June 2025).
Expanded Distribution royalty rate: 40% of list price, same printing-cost deduction.
Printing cost (B&W): flat $2.30 for interiors up to 108 pages; $1.00 + ($0.012 × page count) for 110+ pages.
Formula: royalty = (rate × list price) − printing cost.
WORKED EXAMPLES (60-page B&W coloring book, $2.30 flat printing cost):
Priced at $9.99 → royalty ≈ $3.69/copy on Amazon.com sales (≈ $1.70/copy via Expanded Distribution).
Priced at $12.99 → royalty ≈ $5.49/copy on Amazon.com sales.
Priced at $7.99 (below the 60% threshold) → royalty drops to ≈ $1.70/copy — roughly a $2/copy penalty just for pricing under $9.99.
RULE OF THUMB: whenever page count qualifies for the flat printing-cost tier, price at $9.99 or higher unless a specific niche clearly needs a lower price to compete. The 50%→60% cliff at $9.99 is usually worth more than the extra volume a lower price might win.
ROUGH CATALOG TARGET (planning number, not a promise):
At ≈$3.70/copy net royalty ($9.99 pricing): ≈270 copies/month across the whole catalog (≈9/day) reaches $1,000/month.
At ≈$5.50/copy net royalty ($12.99 pricing): ≈182 copies/month (≈6/day) reaches the same goal.
Real catalogs rarely sell evenly. Plan around a small number of "winner" titles carrying most of the volume, not equal contribution from every book.

ADDENDUM 3 — REVENUE-DRIVEN PRODUCTION LOGIC (refines Section 14)
Layer this feedback loop on top of the existing 2-day production cadence instead of treating every niche as equally worth the same fixed effort.
PHASE 1 — PROBE (first ~6–8 books)
Spread these across different niches/sub-niches from Section 2 (e.g. one cute-animal book, one fantasy-creature book, one seasonal book, one adult-relaxation book) rather than going deep on one series yet. The goal here is signal, not depth.
PHASE 2 — MEASURE (ongoing, ~30 days of sales data per book minimum)
For every published book, log: units/month, royalty/copy, revenue/month, and which category/keywords it's ranking under in the KDP dashboard.
Classify each as WINNER (clearly above-average sales), AVERAGE, or LOSER (near-zero sales after a fair trial period).
PHASE 3 — DOUBLE DOWN
For every WINNER, build out the rest of that series (Section 15) before starting an unrelated new niche. A proven niche with 3–5 books usually outperforms 3–5 unrelated one-off books.
For LOSERs, stop expanding that niche/style and redirect the next production slot to a new probe or a winner's series instead.
PHASE 4 — MAINTAIN
Once cumulative net royalty income is ≥$1,000/month for 2 consecutive months, shift from maximum publishing velocity to "maintain + selectively expand": keep winners refreshed (covers, keywords), reserve the 2-day cadence mainly for series tied to proven winners, and treat brand-new unproven niches as smaller, lower-priority bets.
Add these columns to the Section 14 master production tracker:
LIST PRICE | ROYALTY-RATE TIER | EST. ROYALTY/COPY | UNITS SOLD (30-DAY) | REVENUE (30-DAY) | CLASSIFICATION (WINNER/AVERAGE/LOSER/PROBE) | CUMULATIVE CATALOG REVENUE | GAP TO $1,000 GOAL

ADDENDUM 4 — COLORING PAGE & COVER ART PROMPT LIBRARY
Use these as templates for whichever image-generation tool produces the interior artwork. Fill in the bracketed placeholders per book.
MASTER INTERIOR PROMPT (universal template):
"A single [SUBJECT] in a [SCENE/SETTING], designed as a coloring book page for [AGE GROUP]. Clean black outline line art only, no shading, no gradients, no gray fill, no color. Bold, evenly-weighted lines with fully enclosed regions suitable for coloring. Centered composition with generous white negative space and safe margins on all sides. [SIMPLE / MODERATELY DETAILED / HIGHLY DETAILED] linework appropriate for [young children / older kids / adults]. Pure white background, no text, no watermark, no signature, no frame unless specified, no photorealistic rendering — flat vector-style line art only."
NEGATIVE / EXCLUSION LIST (attach to every interior prompt):
"No color, no shading, no gray tones, no gradients, no text or lettering, no logos or brand marks, no watermark or signature, no cropped or cut-off subject, no extra or malformed limbs, no photorealistic style, no busy or cluttered background."
NICHE VARIANTS (swap into [SUBJECT]/[SCENE]):
Cute forest animals: "a [fox / rabbit / owl / hedgehog] with an oversized head and big friendly eyes, sitting among mushrooms and ferns in a woodland clearing"
Ocean animals: "a [sea turtle / dolphin / octopus / seahorse] swimming among coral and bubbles, gently stylized and rounded shapes"
Fantasy creatures: "a friendly [dragon / unicorn / griffin] with soft rounded features, standing in an enchanted forest clearing with oversized flowers"
Dinosaurs: "a [triceratops / stegosaurus / friendly T-rex] with a gentle expression, standing among ferns with a simplified prehistoric landscape in the background"
Seasonal/holiday: "a [snowman / pumpkin character / spring rabbit] in a [winter / autumn / spring] scene with seasonal props, festive but simple layout"
Adult relaxation (intricate): "a detailed mandala-style composition combining [floral / geometric / animal] motifs, symmetrical, dense linework, no color, full-page pattern"
COVER PROMPT TEMPLATE (distinct from interior — covers may be fully colored even when interior pages are line art):
"A vibrant, colorful, professionally illustrated cover for a coloring book titled '[TITLE]'. Features a friendly [MAIN SUBJECT] as the hero image, in [ART STYLE] with warm inviting colors. Composition leaves clear open space in the upper third for the title and lower third for subtitle/page-count text. No borders resembling official barcodes, no fake awards or review badges, no copyrighted characters or logos."
Difficulty guide: children's books (ages 3–8) → large simple shapes, thick outlines. Family/all-ages → medium detail. Adult relaxation → fine detail, intricate patterns, smaller enclosed regions.
